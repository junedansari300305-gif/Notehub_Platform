About NoteHub

🌟 Our Mission

NoteHub is a free and open platform created to help degree college students access and share study materials easily. Whether it's PDFs, notes, or YouTube tutorials – we aim to bring everything together in one place, built by students, for students.

🚀 Why NoteHub?

During our college days, finding proper notes, references, and video explanations was always a hassle. So we built NoteHub – where students can contribute their own resources and help others. It's fast, free, and designed for simplicity.
